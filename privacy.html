Privacy Policy

VG World Studio · Effective date: 1 November 2026 · Last updated: 1 November 2026

VG World Studio ("the app", "we") is a small private tool, run by Vikas Gaira in India, that manages one Instagram professional account that we own: @shintra.aamdali, a channel of short Hindi stories for families. The app is not offered to the public and does not manage anyone else's account. It uses only Meta's official Instagram API (Instagram API with Instagram Login).

1. What data the app accesses

DataWhy we use it
Our own account's basic profile (username, account ID, follower and post counts) and our own posts (IDs, captions, links)To publish our Reels and to know which post a comment belongs to.
Comments on our own posts (comment text, commenter's username, time, comment ID)To read comments, reply to them, and hide spam or unsafe comments.
Direct messages people send to our account (message text, sender's Instagram-scoped ID and username, time)To answer people who wrote to us first, including a one-time automatic thank-you message. We never send unsolicited messages.
Insights for our own account and posts (views, reach, likes, comments, saves, shares, watch time, follower counts; aggregated numbers only)To see which stories families enjoy and plan better ones.

We do not access anyone's contacts, location, photos, email address, phone number, or other accounts. We do not use Facebook Login and we do not access Facebook data.

2. How replies are made: automatic only under fixed safety rules

When new comments or messages arrive, an AI assistant (Claude, by Anthropic) may draft suggested replies to comments. To do this, the comment or message text and the sender's username are sent to Anthropic.

Simple, polite replies to comments and a one-time thank-you message for new direct messages may be sent automatically under fixed safety rules; anything sensitive is held for a person. A reply is only sent automatically when the comment is clearly friendly (for example praise, thanks or a question about our stories), the reply is short, contains no links and never asks for personal details, and nobody on our side has already answered. Comments or messages with personal details, complaints, health or safety concerns, religion or politics, business requests, or anything unclear are never answered automatically; a person reads and handles them. Obvious spam may be hidden automatically, which can be undone. The thank-you message for direct messages is sent once per conversation and nothing more is sent automatically after it. A person can switch automatic replies off at any time, and every automatic reply is logged so it can be checked and removed. (FAQ answers set up in Instagram's own built-in inbox tools are separate from this app.)

3. Where data is stored and for how long

  • Storage: on one private computer we control, in local files: a log of comments and messages we have handled, a file of draft replies, a log of automatic replies, and a cache of the latest inbox check that is overwritten on every check. This data is not uploaded to any cloud storage.
  • Retention: handled comments and messages, drafts, and the log of automatic replies are deleted automatically after 90 days (sooner if you ask, see section 6). Aggregated insights numbers for our own posts may be kept longer, because they contain no personal information.
  • Security: the computer is password-protected. Our Instagram access token is kept in a private file on that computer and as an encrypted secret in our private GitHub repository, where it is used only to publish our own posts. It is never written to logs or shared.

4. Sharing

  • We do not sell any data, and we do not share it with advertisers, data brokers or anyone else.
  • Service providers that process data for us only to run the app: Meta (the Instagram API itself), Anthropic (drafting suggested replies, see section 2), and GitHub (hosting our own video files while they are being published, and this page; no comments or messages are stored there). They do not receive data for their own advertising.
  • We may disclose data if the law requires it, or to report a risk to a child's safety to Instagram or the authorities.

5. Children's privacy

  • Our stories are made for families. Our audience is parents and grandparents who watch with their children. Instagram accounts are for people aged 13 and over.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. We never ask anyone for a name, age, school, city, photo or contact details, and we never invite a child to message us privately.
  • If a comment contains personal information (for example a child's full name, school, phone number or address), we hide it on Instagram and do not keep it beyond the 90-day limit above.
  • If you are a parent and believe your child shared personal information with us, send a message to our Instagram account @shintra.aamdali and we will delete it.

6. Your choices and deleting your data

  • You can delete your own comment on Instagram at any time, and you can stop messaging us.
  • To ask us to delete any data we hold about you (your comments, messages, or our drafts and replies to them), send a direct message with the words DELETE MY DATA to our Instagram account @shintra.aamdali. We delete it within 30 days and confirm by direct message. Details: data deletion instructions.
  • Deleting data in this app does not delete content on Instagram itself; use Instagram's own settings for that.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
