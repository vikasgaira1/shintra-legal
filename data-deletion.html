Data Deletion Instructions

VG World Studio (Instagram account @shintra.aamdali) · Effective date: 1 November 2026

VG World Studio is a private tool, run by Vikas Gaira, that manages only our own Instagram account, @shintra.aamdali. It may hold the text of comments you left on our posts or messages you sent us, together with your Instagram username, and the replies we drafted or sent. We delete these automatically after 90 days. See our Privacy Policy. Here is how to have them deleted sooner.

Option 1: send us a DM on Instagram

  1. Open our Instagram account @shintra.aamdali and tap Message.
  2. Send us a DM with the words DELETE MY DATA. Please don't send any other personal details; your Instagram username is enough for us to find your comments and messages.
  3. We delete every comment, message, draft and reply record linked to your username from our app's storage within 30 days, and confirm by DM.
To: @shintra.aamdali (Instagram direct message)
DELETE MY DATA

Option 2: remove the data on Instagram yourself

  • Delete your comment on our post (press and hold the comment, then Delete). Our app stops seeing it.
  • Delete the chat on your side. Note that this removes it only from your inbox; to remove our copy, use option 1.

What is not affected

Aggregated statistics about our own posts (for example "120 views") contain no personal data and are kept. Content on Instagram itself is controlled by Instagram's own settings.
